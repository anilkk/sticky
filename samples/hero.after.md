## Find out why customers churn without reading every call transcript

Fieldnote reads your sales and support calls and lists every mention of a competitor, a missing
feature, or a reason to cancel. Each one comes with the exact quote and a link to that moment in
the recording.

For product marketers who currently tag calls by hand in a spreadsheet.
